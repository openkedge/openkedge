## Deliverable B — Summary of Formal Repairs

1. **Theorem 1 (Latent-Score Conformity Covariance Contraction):** Defined over unbounded latent score residuals $z_i^{(t)} \in \mathbb{R}$, $\varepsilon_i^{(t)} = z_i^{(t)} - z^\star(S_\tau) \in \mathbb{R}$, with nonlinear probability readout $y_i^{(t)} = \psi(z_i^{(t)}) \in [0,1]$. Unbounded $\lambda_\parallel^{(t)} = \Theta(t)$ holds strictly in latent score space; probability-valued beliefs have $\operatorname{Var}(y_i^{(t)} \mid S_\tau) \le 1/4$ and $\lambda_\parallel^{(t)} \le N/4$ unconditionally.
2. **Theorem 3 (Complete-Proof Independence & Positive Evidence Certification):** Anchored explicitly to a consistent snapshot $S_{v_{\mathrm{snap}}}$; states both the deterministic $k_a$-domain fault threshold and its contrapositive: under Assumptions 1–2 and $\le f = k_a - 1$ faulty failure domains, $\hat{\nu}_{\mathrm{pack}}(\mathcal{W}(a)) \ge k_a \implies \nu_{\mathrm{pack}}(\mathcal{W}(a)) \ge k_a \implies c_a$ physically holds in $S_{v_{\mathrm{snap}}}$.
3. **Lemma 4 (Fault-Tolerant Plausible-World Invariant Safety — Independent of Positive Packing):** Under Assumptions 2–3 (with Assumption 1 implying Assumption 3(2) for Horn refutations) at consistent snapshot $v_{\mathrm{snap}}$ with $\le f$ faulty domains: (i) refutation packing $\hat{\nu}_{\mathrm{pack}}(\mathcal{W}_{\mathrm{refute}}(s)) \le f$ retains $S_{v_{\mathrm{snap}}} \in \mathcal{B}^{(f)} \subseteq \widehat{\mathcal{B}}^{(f)}$ regardless of truncation; (ii) $a \in \bigcap_{s \in \widehat{\mathcal{B}}^{(f)}} A_{\mathrm{safe}}^{\mathcal{K}}(s)$ guarantees $a \in A_{\mathrm{safe}}^{\mathcal{K}}(S_{v_{\mathrm{snap}}})$ and $\Post(S_{v_{\mathrm{snap}}}, a) \subseteq \mathcal{K}$ **without requiring $\nu_{\mathrm{pack}}(\mathcal{W}(a)) \ge k_a$**.
4. **Theorem 5 (Closed-Domain Physical Invariant Preservation):** Drops the unused $\nu_{\mathrm{pack}}(\mathcal{W}(a)) \ge k_a$ premise; requires the **contiguous commit-version barrier** (both $\Delta_C^\bot(\mathcal{D}) = \varnothing$ and no missing commit version in $(v_{\min}, v_{\mathrm{head}}]$); requires linearizable $\mathbf{r}_{\mathrm{adm}}(\mathcal{D})$ CAS for **both Mode S and Mode C** admissions (invalidated on any ledger state mutation); and lifts abstract schedule safety to physical traces $\theta$ via explicit stuttering-refinement contract $\mathsf{Refines}_{\mathcal{D}}$.
5. **Corollary 1 (Compositional Execution-Bound Admission):** Explicitly composes the four independent obligations: (1) Positive Evidence Certification ($\mathsf{EvidenceValid} \implies c_a(S_{v_{\mathrm{snap}}})$ holds), (2) Plausible-World & Concurrent Physical Invariant Preservation ($\mathsf{SafetyValid}_{\mathcal{D}} \implies \forall k,\, \alpha(\sigma_k) \in \mathcal{K}_{\mathcal{D}} \land \sigma_k \in \mathcal{K}_{\mathcal{D}}^{\mathrm{phys}}$ and $\Exp_{\mathrm{real}} \le B$), (3) Execution-Time Authorization Freshness ($\mathsf{AuthorizationValid}$ at $t_{\mathrm{eff}}$), and (4) Durable Target Enforcement (Tier E2 atomic commit at the physical effect boundary, or Tier E1+-quiescence post-$\mathsf{FenceAck}$).
6. **Proposition 6 (Conditional Eight-Risk Stratum Accounting):** Clarifies $E_4$ as an exogenous cross-domain common-cause fault mechanism and disambiguates $D = |\mathcal{F}|$ from $\mathcal{D} = \Dom^\star(a)$.

## Deliverable G — Residual Limitations (Explicit Non-Guarantees)

Without the following external preconditions, PIEQ **cannot** guarantee safety or liveness:
1. **Trusted Domain Models ($\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{sys}}, P_{\mathrm{sys}}, \mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{cand}}, A_{\mathrm{safe}}, \widehat{\Post}, \mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{inv}}$):** Omitted fault worlds ($E_1$), unsound deductive or transition rules ($E_2$), or omitted invariant couplings ($E_8$) can admit physically unsafe mutations despite $k_a$ disjoint proofs.
2. **Complete Physical Failure-Domain Classification ($h_{\mathrm{man}}$):** Misclassified shared physical dependencies ($E_3$) or unmodeled cross-domain common-cause faults ($E_4$) defeat deterministic fault counting ($E_5$).
3. **Physical-to-Abstract Refinement ($\mathsf{Refines}_{\mathcal{D}}$):** Finite phase-graph checking proves safety of the abstract model only; unmodeled internal phases, cached reads, background callbacks, recovery continuations, or non-idempotent retries invalidate R1/R2 unless formally discharged.
4. **Target-Native Enforcement & Complete Writer Mediation:** Durable local deduplication does not make a nontransactional external actuator idempotent; out-of-band external writers ($E_6$) bypass $L_{\mathrm{exp}}$; non-lease-fenced E1+ targets trade liveness for safety via indefinite `UNKNOWN` blocking on stall/partition.
5. **Empirical Scope & Prover-Liveness Gap:** Current empirical evidence comprises unit/regression suites, a scripted 100-scenario fixture, single-host POSIX/SQLite and local 3-member `etcd` fault trials, and one unpinned-identity live LLM pilot showing `0/40` autonomous mitigations without interactive probing. End-to-end distributed multi-node database validation with live LLMs (Deliverable E) remains unexecuted.
